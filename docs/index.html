When to book

  • Long-haul from Europe is usually cheapest about 1–4 months before departure; some studies say up to 8 months.
  • Inside Europe, about 1–3 months ahead is typical.
  • Christmas, New Year, Easter and summer holidays: book earlier, around 4–8 months out.
  • Real deals and error fares disappear in hours, so book first and plan after (most EU bookings can't be cancelled for free, so check the fare rules).

Where the prices come from

A scanner runs every 30 minutes on GitHub, for free. It asks the Travelpayouts / Aviasales data service for the cheapest return flights from Copenhagen to everywhere for the next 10 months.

These prices come from searches other travellers made in the last few days, so they can be a little out of date. Always check the price on Google Flights or Skyscanner before you book.

It also checks other countries' versions of Aviasales ("markets"), because they can list different travel agencies and prices. Each deal shows which market had the lowest price.

Prices are per person, return, economy, in DKK. Made for personal use.
